01 / about

Engineer by training, analyst by choice.

I started on the systems side — Kafka pipelines, cloud schedulers, a lot of C++. Then I moved into pharma analytics: rare disease at AstraZeneca, and now HIV commercial operations at ViiV Healthcare, where I work on territory goals, rank reports and incentive payouts.

The common thread is taking messy data and turning it into something a sales director or brand lead can actually act on. The longer-term goal is to keep doing that at a bigger scale, using technology to change how healthcare works.

Outside of work I'm usually on a field — playing, or refereeing as a RecWell supervisor at UMD — watching football (the round-ball kind), playing guitar badly, or planning the next trip.
